### **Color**

A wavelength of **550 nm** corresponds to the **green** part of the visible spectrum. This is right in the middle of the range of colors humans can see, which is why our eyes are so efficient at detecting it.

---

### **Frequency ($f$)**

To find the frequency, we use the formula $c = f \lambda$, where $c$ is the speed of light ($3 \times 10^8$ m/s).

1.  **Convert wavelength to meters:
   ** $550 \text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$.
3.  **Calculate:**
   
    $$f = \frac{c}{\lambda} = \frac{3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}}$$
    
5.  **Result:**
   
    **$f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$**
