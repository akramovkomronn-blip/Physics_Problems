To find the speed of light in a medium, you use the formula for the index of refraction:

$$v = \frac{c}{n}$$

### **The Variables**
*   **$c$ (Speed of light in vacuum):** $3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$
  
*   **$n$ (Index of refraction for diamond):** $2.42$

### **The Calculation**
$$v = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{2.42}$$

$$v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$$

### **Final Answer**

The speed of light in a diamond is approximately **$124,000,000 \text{ m/s}$** (or $1.24 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$). 

This is significantly slower than its speed in a vacuum—only about **41%** of its maximum speed—because the high optical density of diamond slows the light down considerably.
