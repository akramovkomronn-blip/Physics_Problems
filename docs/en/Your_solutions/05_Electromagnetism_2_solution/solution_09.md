To find the angle of refraction, we use **Snell's Law**:

$$n_1 \sin(\theta_1) = n_2 \sin(\theta_2)$$

### **The Variables**

*   **$n_1$ (Air):** $1.00$
  
*   **$\theta_1$ (Incidence):** $30^\circ$
  
*   **$n_2$ (Glass):** $1.50$
  
*   **$\theta_2$ (Refraction):** Unknown

---

### **The Calculation**

1.  **Set up the equation:**
   
    $1.00 \times \sin(30^\circ) = 1.50 \times \sin(\theta_2)$

3.  **Solve for $\sin(\theta_2)$:**
   
    Since $\sin(30^\circ) = 0.5$:

    $0.5 = 1.50 \times \sin(\theta_2)$
    
    $\sin(\theta_2) = \frac{0.5}{1.50} = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$

5.  **Find the angle:**
   
    $\theta_2 = \arcsin(0.333)$

### **Final Answer**

The angle of refraction is approximately **$19.47^\circ$**.
