To list these by **increasing wavelength** (from the shortest waves to the longest waves), the order is:

1.  **Gamma rays** (Shortest wavelength, highest energy)
2.  **X-rays**
3.  **Ultraviolet**
4.  **Infrared**
5.  **Microwaves**
6.  **Radio waves** (Longest wavelength, lowest energy)



In this order, as the wavelength gets **longer**, the frequency and the energy of the radiation **decrease**.
