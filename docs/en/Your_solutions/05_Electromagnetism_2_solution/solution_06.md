### **1. Direction**

The wave moves in the **positive x-direction**.

*(Tip: In the expression $$(kx - \omega t)$$, the minus sign means it's moving forward.)*

### **2. Wavelength ($\lambda$)**

Using $k = 10^7$:

$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2\pi}{10^7} \approx 6.28 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m}$$

### **3. Angular Frequency ($\omega$)**

Using $\omega = c \cdot k$:

$$\omega = (3 \times 10^8) \times 10^7 = 3 \times 10^{15} \text{ rad/s}$$

### **4. Magnetic Field ($B_z$)**

The magnetic field is perpendicular to the electric field ($E_y$) and the direction of travel ($x$). This places it on the **z-axis**.

*   **Amplitude:** $B_0 = \frac{E_0}{c} = \frac{100}{3 \times 10^8} \approx 3.33 \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}$

**The equation is:**

$$B_z(x,t) = 3.33 \times 10^{-7} \sin(10^7 x - 3 \times 10^{15} t) \text{ T}$$
