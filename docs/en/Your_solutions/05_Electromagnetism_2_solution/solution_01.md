
### Gauss's Law Formula
The total electric flux $\Phi_E$ through any closed surface is given by:

$$\Phi_E = \frac{Q_{enclosed}}{\epsilon_0}$$

Where:
*   $Q_{enclosed}$ is the net charge inside the surface.
*   $\epsilon_0$ is the vacuum permittivity constant, approximately $8.854 \times 10^{-12} \text{ C}^2/(\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2)$.

---

### Calculation
Given that the point charge of $+2 \text{ C}$ is at the origin and the sphere is centered at the origin, the entire charge is enclosed within the radius.

1.  **Identify the enclosed charge:** $Q = 2 \text{ C}$
2.  **Apply the formula:**

$$\Phi_E = \frac{2}{8.854 \times 10^{-12}}$$

3.  **Result:**
$$\Phi_E \approx 2.26 \times 10^{11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
