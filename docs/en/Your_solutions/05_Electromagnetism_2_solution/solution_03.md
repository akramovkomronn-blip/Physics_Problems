
### The Formula
For a small current element $d\mathbf{l}$, the magnetic field $dB$ at a point $P$ is given by:

$$dB = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{I \cdot dl \cdot \sin(\theta)}{r^2}$$

Where:
*   $I = 3 \text{ A}$ (Current)
  
*   $dl = 0.1 \text{ m}$ (Length of the segment)
  
*   $r = 0.2 \text{ m}$ (Distance to point $P$)
  
*   $\theta = 90^\circ$ (Since the segment is perpendicular to the line connecting it to $P$, $\sin(90^\circ) = 1$)
  
*   $\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} = 10^{-7} \text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$

---

### Calculation

1.  **Set up the equation:**
    $$B = 10^{-7} \times \frac{3 \times 0.1 \times 1}{(0.2)^2}$$

2.  **Simplify the denominator:**
    $$(0.2)^2 = 0.04$$

3.  **Solve for B:**
   
    $$B = 10^{-7} \times \frac{0.3}{0.04}$$
    
    $$B = 10^{-7} \times 7.5$$

5.  **Final Value:**
    $$B = 7.5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}$$

---

### Final Result
The magnitude of the magnetic field at point $P$ is **$7.5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}$**

The direction would be determined by the right-hand rule, pointing perpendicular to both the current direction and the vector $r$.
