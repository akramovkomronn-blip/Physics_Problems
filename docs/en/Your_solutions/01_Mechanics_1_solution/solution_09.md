To determine which object has greater momentum, we use the standard formula for momentum:
**Momentum ($p$) = Mass ($m$) × Velocity ($v$)**

Before calculating, we must ensure the units match. We will convert grams to kilograms ($1,000 \text{ g} = 1 \text{ kg}$).

---

### 1. The Fly
* **Mass ($m_1$):** $2 \text{ g} = 0.002 \text{ kg}$
* **Velocity ($v_1$):** $10 \text{ m/s}$
* **Calculation:** $0.002 \times 10 = \mathbf{0.02 \text{ kg·m/s}}$

### 2. The Tennis Ball
* **Mass ($m_2$):** $60 \text{ g} = 0.06 \text{ kg}$
* **Velocity ($v_2$):** $1 \text{ m/s}$
* **Calculation:** $0.06 \times 1 = \mathbf{0.06 \text{ kg·m/s}}$



---

### **The Winner**
The **tennis ball** has greater momentum. 

Even though the fly is moving $10$ times faster, the tennis ball is $30$ times heavier. In the world of momentum, mass usually "wins" unless the smaller object is moving at an extreme speed.
