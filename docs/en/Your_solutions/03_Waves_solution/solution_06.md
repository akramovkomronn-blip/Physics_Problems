### Wave Equation Analysis


The general form of a traveling wave equation is:


$$y(x,t) = A\sin(kx - \omega t)$$


Given equation:


$$y(x,t) = 0.05\sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$$


---

#### a) Amplitude ($A$)


The amplitude is the coefficient in front of the sine function.


**$A = 0.05\text{ m}$**

#### b) Wavelength ($\lambda$)

The wave number $k$ is the coefficient of $x$. From the equation, $k = 2\pi$.
Using the relation $k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$:
$$2\pi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$
**$\lambda = 1.0\text{ m}$**

#### c) Frequency ($f$)

The angular frequency $\omega$ is the coefficient of $t$. From the equation, $\omega = 50\pi$.
Using the relation $\omega = 2\pi f$:
$$50\pi = 2\pi f$$
$$f = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$
**$f = 25\text{ Hz}$**

#### d) Wave Speed ($v$)

The wave speed can be found using $v = f\lambda$ or $v = \frac{\omega}{k}$.
$$v = 25\text{ Hz} \cdot 1.0\text{ m}$$
**$v = 25\text{ m/s}$**
