### Phase Difference Calculation


To find the phase difference ($\Delta \phi$) between two points separated by a distance ($\Delta x$), we use the relationship between spatial separation and wave cycle:

#### 1. Formula

The phase difference is proportional to the fraction of the wavelength ($\lambda$) the distance represents:


$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \Delta x$$



---

#### 2. Calculation
**Given:**


* Path difference ($\Delta x$) = $\frac{\lambda}{3}$

**Substitution:**

$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \left(\frac{\lambda}{3}\right)$$


$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{3}$$

---

#### Final Answer

**$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{ radians}$** (or approximately $2.09\text{ rad}$)
