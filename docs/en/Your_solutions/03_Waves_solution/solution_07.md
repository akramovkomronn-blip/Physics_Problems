### Standing Wave Modes: Wavelength Calculation


In a standing wave on a string fixed at both ends, the number of antinodes corresponds to the harmonic number ($n$).


#### 1. Identify the Harmonic

* **Number of antinodes ($n$):** 4
  
* This means the string is vibrating in its **fourth harmonic**.



#### 2. Relationship between Length ($L$) and Wavelength ($\lambda$)

For any harmonic $n$, the length of the string is related to the wavelength by:

$$L = n\frac{\lambda}{2}$$

Solving for $\lambda$:

$$\lambda = \frac{2L}{n}$$

#### 3. Calculation

**Given:**

* $L = 80\text{ cm} = 0.80\text{ m}$
  
* $n = 4$

$$\lambda = \frac{2 \cdot 0.80}{4}$$

$$\lambda = \frac{1.60}{4}$$

**$\lambda = 0.40\text{ m}$** (or $40\text{ cm}$)

---

#### Final Answer

The wavelength of the wave is

**$0.40\text{ meters}$**.
