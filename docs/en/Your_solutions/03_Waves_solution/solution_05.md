### Echo Ranging: Distance Calculation


To find the distance to the cliff, we use the relationship between speed, distance, and time. In an echo, the sound travels to the cliff and back, covering the distance twice.

#### 1. Formula


The total distance traveled by the sound ($2d$) is equal to the speed of sound ($v$) multiplied by the total time ($t$):


$$2d = v \cdot t \implies d = \frac{v \cdot t}{2}$$




---

#### 2. Calculation

**Given:**

* Speed of sound ($v$): $343\text{ m/s}$


* Total time ($t$): $1.0\text{ s}$

**Substitution:**

$$d = \frac{343 \cdot 1.0}{2}$$

$$d = \frac{343}{2}$$

**$d = 171.5\text{ m}$**


---

#### Final Answer

The cliff is **$171.5\text{ meters}$** away.
