## Harmonic Motion Solution

Given:
x(t) = 0.2 cos(10πt)

Compare with standard SHM:
x(t) = A cos(ωt)

So:
A = 0.2 m  
ω = 10π rad/s  
m = 10 kg  

---

### 1. Spring Constant (k)

Formula:
ω = √(k/m) ⇒ k = mω²

k = 10 × (10π)²  
k = 10 × 100π²  
k = 1000π² ≈ 9870 N/m  

---

### 2. Total Mechanical Energy (E)

Formula:
E = (1/2) k A²

E = (1/2) × 1000π² × (0.2)²  
E = (1/2) × 1000π² × 0.04  
E = 20π² ≈ 197 J  

---

### Final Answers

k ≈ 9870 N/m  
E ≈ 197 J
