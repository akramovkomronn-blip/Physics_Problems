Given:
[
x(t) = 0.2 \cos(10\pi t)
]

So:

* Amplitude (A = 0.2 , \text{m})
* Angular frequency (\omega = 10\pi)

---

### **1. Spring constant (k)**

[
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \Rightarrow k = m \omega^2
]

[
k = 10 \cdot (10\pi)^2 = 10 \cdot 100\pi^2 = 1000\pi^2 \approx 9870 , \text{N/m}
]

---

### **2. Total mechanical energy**

[
E = \frac{1}{2} k A^2
]

[
E = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1000\pi^2 \cdot (0.2)^2 = 20\pi^2 \approx 197 , \text{J}
]

---

### **Final answers**

* **(k \approx 9870 , \text{N/m})**
* **(E \approx 197 , \text{J})**
