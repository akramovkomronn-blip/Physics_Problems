For a moving charge in a magnetic field, the force is given by the Lorentz force:

$[
F = q , v , B , \sin(\theta)
]$

Since the particle enters **perpendicular** to the field,
$[
\theta = 90^\circ \Rightarrow \sin(90^\circ) = 1
]$

---

### Substitute the values:

$[
q = 2 \times 10^{-19} , \text{C}, \quad v = 1 \times 10^{6} , \text{m/s}, \quad B = 0.5 , \text{T}
]$

$[
F = (2 \times 10^{-19}) \times (1 \times 10^{6}) \times (0.5)
]$

---

### Calculate:

$[
F = 1 \times 10^{-13} , \text{N}
]$

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

**( F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} , \text{N} )**
